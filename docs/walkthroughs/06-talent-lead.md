# 📋 How work actually flows

Before the screens, the shape of the thing. Everything in Konma sits in three levels:

**Mission → Quest → Task**

- A **Mission** is a big goal, six to nine months out. *"Standardise all core recipes."*
- A **Quest** is one week of that mission, with an owner and a target date. Quests move **Planned → Active → In Review → Complete**.
- A **Task** is one day's work, owned by one person. Tasks move **To Do → Doing → Done**, and can be **Blocked** or **Cancelled** along the way.

**A task is not finished when someone says it is finished.** It is finished when it is **validated** — and validation needs three things to be true at once:

1. **Status is Done**
2. **At least one evidence approved**
3. **All required approvals satisfied**

You will see exactly those three lines, with ticks, on every task page. Only when all three are ticked does the XP land and the readiness meter move.

> 💡 **This is the single most useful thing you can teach a new joiner.** "Done" is a claim. "Validated" is a fact. The gap between them is where most new people get stuck, and closing it is a training problem — which makes it yours.

Sidebar → **Mission Control**. The page is titled **My Day**.

This is a read-only briefing. There is nothing to click that changes anything — it just tells you where you stand.

## The five sections

**Today's focus** — what is due on you. When there is nothing: **"Nothing is due on you."** — *"Work lands here when a quest is assigned to you or a task you own reaches its due date."* with a **Browse all tasks** button. Anything late is marked **· Overdue**.

**My quest** — the quest you are on this week, with a **Quest progress** bar and a line like *"2 of 5 of your tasks in this quest are done"*. When you are not on one: **"No quest is active for you this week."** with **Browse missions**.

**Nudges** — one-line prompts that only appear when something needs you. They are worth reading; they are the system doing your chasing for you:

- *"[task] has been overdue for 3 days — close it or move the date."* → **Open task**
- *"Evidence you uploaded 4 days ago is still unreviewed — your XP will not land until it is approved."* → **Chase review**
- *"[quest] is 30% done with 1 day left in the window."* → **Open quest**

> 💡 Start here every morning. If the Nudges section is empty, your day is genuinely clear.

# ✅ My Tasks

Sidebar → **My Tasks**. The page title is **My Tasks**.

## Two ways to look

Top right, a toggle between **Board** and **List**.

- **Board** shows four columns — **To Do**, **Doing**, **Done**, **Blocked**. Best for "what am I actually doing".
- **List** shows a table with the columns **Title**, **Status**, **Priority**, **Owner**, **Due Date**, **Quest**, **Mission**, **XP**, **Blocked**. Best for "what is the state of everything".

## Filtering

The bar under the title, left to right:

1. **Mine** — a toggle. It is on by default, and the heading says **My Tasks**. Turning it off changes the heading to **All Tasks**.
2. **Any status** — tick any of **To Do**, **Doing**, **Blocked**, **Done**, **Cancelled**.
3. **Any quest** — narrow to one quest.
4. **Any type** — **Core**, **Ad-hoc** or **Improvement**.
5. **Any priority** — **Low**, **Medium**, **High**, **Critical**.
6. **Clear** — appears once a filter is on.

On the right, a running count: **"7 tasks"**.

> ⚠️ **Turning "Mine" off will not show you other people's tasks.** The heading changes to **All Tasks**, but the results stay yours — the system still filters to what you own. To see a teammate's work, open the **quest** it belongs to (see below), where every task on that quest is listed whoever owns it.

Empty result: **"Nothing matches these filters."**
Load failure: **"Could not load tasks"** — *"The task list did not come back. Try again in a moment."* with **Retry**.

## The XP column

Two ways it can read:

- **`+18 XP`** in grey — what this task *would* be worth. Potential, not banked.
- **`25 XP`** in green — what it *is* worth. Validated and counted.

That colour change is the whole game.

# 🔍 Working one task

Click a task title to open it.

## What is on the page

The heading is the task title, with badges for status, type and priority. Underneath, cards in this order:

| Card | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| **Overview** | The description, its domain, its XP value, when it is due |
| **People** | Who owns it |
| **Status** | The status control, and the blocker box |
| **Dependencies** | Any task that must finish before this one can |
| **Evidence** | Uploads, and the validation checklist |

At the top of the Evidence card sits **Validation required**, a ring showing **0/3**, **1/3**, **2/3** or **3/3**, and the three lines:

- **Status is Done**
- **At least one evidence approved**
- **All required approvals satisfied**

Once all three are ticked the card flips to **Valid** and **Task validated**, and the ring reads **3/3**.

## Moving a task along

1. In the **Status** card, use the dropdown. On your own tasks you can pick **To Do**, **Doing** or **Done**.
2. Set it to **Doing** when you actually start. It is not bureaucracy — it is what stops someone else picking up the same job.

> ⚠️ If you open a task that is not yours you will see **"Read-only — this task belongs to [name]"**. That is expected.

## Reporting a blocker

When something outside your control stops you, say so — do not let the task sit silently.

1. In the **Status** card, click **Report blocker**.
2. The dialog says: *"What is blocking this task? Be specific so your admin can help resolve it."*
3. Fill in **Blocker reason**. The hint suggests *"e.g. Waiting for supplier confirmation on ingredient list"*. At least three characters, but write a real sentence.
4. Click **Report blocker**.

The task turns red with a **Blocked** badge and your reason, and it appears in the admin's blockers list. When the blocker clears, an admin uses **Resolve blocker** to release it.

> 💡 **A blocked task with a good reason is a healthy task. A stalled task with no reason is an invisible one.** This is the habit worth drilling into every new joiner in their first week.

# 📎 Evidence

Evidence is the proof a task was actually done. Photos, screenshots, documents, a link, or a written note.

## Uploading it

On a task you own, scroll to the **Evidence** card. You get a drop zone reading **"Drop files here, or click to browse"** and **"Photos, documents, video up to 10 MB"**, plus three buttons: **Upload evidence**, **Add a link**, **Add a note**.

**A file:**

1. Drag it in, or click to browse.
2. It uploads and goes straight to **Pending**.

**A link** (useful for a shared document or a training video):

1. Click **Add a link**.
2. Paste the address in the **https://...** box.
3. Add context in the box marked *"Add context for the reviewer"*.
4. Click **Save link** → **"Link evidence submitted."**

**A note** (useful when the proof is a conversation, not a file):

1. Click **Add a note**.
2. Write in the box: *"Describe what you did, including any relevant details..."*
3. Click **Save note** → **"Note submitted."**

There is also a paperclip button on every task row and board card. It opens a panel titled **Add evidence** with tabs **File**, **Link** and **Note**, and the line: *"Attach proof to [task]. Evidence is what turns the task's XP from potential into earned."*

**Limits:** 10 MB, photos, documents or video. Too big gives *"That file is too large — keep it under 10 MB."*; wrong kind gives *"That file type isn't supported — try a photo, document, or video."*

## What happens next

Each upload shows a status badge: **Pending**, **Approved** or **Rejected**. A rejected item shows the reviewer's reason as **"Rejected: [notes]"** — read it, fix what they asked for, and upload again.

When the last of the three validation conditions falls into place, you get confetti and a message: **"Task validated! +25 XP"**.

## The Evidence Feed

Sidebar → **Evidence**. The page is titled **Evidence Feed**.

**This is the whole team's evidence, not just yours** — and for your role it is one of the most useful pages in the system.

Filter with **All**, **Pending**, **Approved** and **Rejected**. Each card shows the task title, the status badge, who uploaded it (or *"Auto-captured by the mission bridge"*), how long ago, and which quest it belongs to. **Load more evidence** at the bottom.

Empty: **"No evidence submitted"** — *"Evidence submitted to tasks will appear here."*

> 💡 **Click "Pending" and sort out what you are looking at.** A long pending list means reviewers are the bottleneck. A long **Rejected** list means people are submitting weak evidence — and that is a training gap you can close. Reading this one filter every few days tells you more about how the team is working than any status meeting.

# 🗳️ Decisions

Sidebar → **Decisions**. Titled **Decisions**.

This is the team's memory — what was decided, why, and who agreed.

## Reading it

Tabs across the top: **All**, **Proposed**, **Aligned**, **Approved**, **Rejected**, **Reopened**. A search box reads *"Search decisions..."*.

Each decision shows its title, type (**Individual**, **Cross-function** or **Strategic**), and status. Open one and you get **Context**, the roles required to agree, and how each of them voted.

Empty: **"No decisions yet"**.

## Voting

**When a decision names your role, you get a vote — and this is where you have real say.** Hiring, training and onboarding decisions are exactly the ones your role belongs on.

1. Open the decision and read the **Context**.
2. At the bottom, three buttons: **Approve**, **Reject**, **Abstain**.
3. **Approve** or **Abstain** record straight away — **"Vote recorded: approved."**
4. **Reject** asks you why first, in a box reading *"What makes this the wrong call right now?"*. Write something the proposer can act on.

If you have already voted you will see: *"You voted approved — voting again replaces it."* You can change your mind until the decision is resolved.

If your role is not on a decision, the buttons are simply absent. That is normal — not every decision needs everybody.

> ⚠️ The **Log Decision** button at the top will not work for you yet. Saving shows *"Couldn't log that decision — check the fields and try again."* To get a decision recorded, ask the Founder/Admin or a lead who holds the permission.

Sidebar → **Readiness**. Titled **Readiness Intelligence**, subtitled *"Validated tasks and live operations data, blended into one number per meter."*

Meters are grouped into two sections:

**Derived from operations** — *"Computed from live ops data — catalog, procurement, sales and quality — and recomputed nightly."*

**Task-driven** — *"Moves only when a task is validated with approved evidence."*

# ⭐ XP and levels

**XP is only ever earned by validated tasks.** There is no other route. Doing the work is not enough; the work has to be done, evidenced, and approved.

## How much a task is worth

Every task carries a base XP value. What lands depends on its type:

| Type | You keep | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Core** | **100%** | Planned work, on the roadmap |
| **Improvement** | **80%** | Good work, but you chose it yourself |
| **Ad-hoc** | **70%** | Urgent firefighting — necessary, but it should not out-earn the plan |

So a 25 XP core task pays 25. The same task marked ad-hoc pays 17.

## Levels

| Level | XP needed |
| --- | --- |
| **Lvl 1** | 0 – 199 |
| **Lvl 2** | 200 – 499 |
| **Lvl 3** | 500 – 999 |
| **Lvl 4** | 1000 and above |

Your XP chip in the header shows your total and your level, like `340 XP  Lvl 2`. Click it for a bar reading **"Level 2 — 340/500 XP to next"**.

Crossing a threshold gives you a full-screen **Level Up!** moment and the message **"Level up! You're now Level 3."**

> 💡 **What to do with all this as the Talent Lead.** XP is not a stick. Use it as a diagnostic. Someone whose XP has not moved in three weeks is either blocked, has no tasks assigned, or is submitting evidence nobody reviews — and all three are fixable. Check **Team → Contribution**, then **Evidence Feed → Pending**, then talk to them. In that order.

# ✅ Worked example — take one task all the way, and watch the leaderboard move

Do this once end to end. It teaches you the loop from the inside, which is the only way to teach it to anyone else. Allow about twenty minutes plus a wait for a reviewer.

**The goal:** move one task from To Do to validated, and see your XP and your leaderboard position change as a result.

- [ ] **1. Write down your starting numbers.** Look at the **XP chip** in the header — note the number and the level. Then Sidebar → **Team** → **Leaderboard** tab, and note your **Rank** and **XP** on the row marked **(you)**.

- [ ] **2. Pick a task.** Sidebar → **My Tasks**. Switch to **Board** view. Pick something in **To Do** that you can genuinely finish today. Note its **XP** value and its type badge (**Core**, **Ad-hoc** or **Improvement**) — that tells you what it will pay.

- [ ] **3. Open it and start it.** Click the title. In the **Status** card, change the dropdown to **Doing**. Look at the **Evidence** card: the ring says **0/3** and all three lines are unticked. That is your scoreboard for this task.

- [ ] **4. Do the actual work.** Genuinely. The whole system falls apart the moment anyone treats this as paperwork.

- [ ] **5. Attach the proof.** Back on the task, in the **Evidence** card:
  - If you have a photo or document, drop it into **"Drop files here, or click to browse"**.
  - If the proof is a document elsewhere, click **Add a link**, paste it, and use the *"Add context for the reviewer"* box to say what they are looking at.
  - If there is no file at all, click **Add a note** and write plainly what you did — *"Describe what you did, including any relevant details..."*. Aim for three sentences: what you did, how you know it worked, anything the reviewer should notice.
  - Confirm you see **"Note submitted."** (or the equivalent) and the item appears with a **Pending** badge.

- [ ] **6. Mark it Done.** In the **Status** card, change the dropdown to **Done**. Check the Evidence card again — the ring should now read **1/3** or **2/3**, with **Status is Done** ticked.

- [ ] **7. Understand what you are waiting on.** Look at which of the three lines are still unticked:
  - **At least one evidence approved** — a reviewer has to look at your upload. That is gate one.
  - **All required approvals satisfied** — if the task requires sign-off, a named role has to approve it too. That is gate two, and it is separate.
  Most tasks in the food domain need **two** people to sign off, not one. That is deliberate, and it is why "I finished it" and "it counted" are different days.

- [ ] **8. Chase the review — properly.** Go to Sidebar → **Evidence**, click the **Pending** filter, and find your card. Now message the reviewer with something specific: *"[task name] is done and the evidence is up — could you take a look when you get a moment?"* Vague chasing gets ignored; naming the task does not.

- [ ] **9. When it is approved, watch the payoff.** Reopen the task. The Evidence card should now read **Valid** and **Task validated**, with the ring at **3/3**. If you were on the page when it happened you will have seen confetti and **"Task validated! +25 XP"**.

- [ ] **10. Check your XP moved.** Look at the header chip. It should be higher than the number you wrote down in step 1, by the task's effective value — full value for a **Core** task, 80% for **Improvement**, 70% for **Ad-hoc**. Click the chip and read the bar: **"Level 2 — 365/500 XP to next"**.

- [ ] **11. Check the leaderboard moved.** Sidebar → **Team** → **Leaderboard**. Your **XP** should match the chip. Your **Rank** may or may not have changed — it only moves when you pass someone, which is worth understanding before you explain the leaderboard to anyone else.

- [ ] **12. Check the readiness meter moved.** Sidebar → **Readiness**. Find the meter your task was mapped to — if it was talent work, **Talent Readiness**. Click its ring and look at **Contributing tasks**. Your task should now be in that list with its XP beside it. **That list is the proof that the meter is real** and not a number somebody typed in.

- [ ] **13. See it land publicly.** Sidebar → **Team** → **Wins** tab. Your task should appear as **[Task Validated]** with your name on it.

- [ ] **14. Now do the same trip for someone else.** Pick a teammate. Open **Team → Contribution** and find their role. Compare **tasks completed** against **validated** — if there is a gap, go to **Evidence → Pending** and see whether their work is sitting unreviewed. You have just done the core of your job: found a person who is stuck, and found out exactly where.
