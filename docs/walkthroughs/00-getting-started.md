# 🔑 Signing in

Everyone on the team signs in at the same place.

1. Open **https://www.konma.store/team** in your browser.
2. In the **Email** box, type your work address — it ends in `@konma.store`.
3. In the **Password** box, type the password you set.
4. Click **Sign in**.

You land on your dashboard.

**The very first time**, you don't have a password yet. The admin adds you to the team and the system emails you a link. Open that email and click through to the **Set your password** page. Pick a password with **at least 8 characters** and **at least one number**, then click **Set password and sign in**.

> 💡 The little eye icon at the right of the password box shows what you typed. Handy if the password is long.

**If you forget your password**, click **Forgot password?** on the sign-in page, type your email, and check your inbox. The link expires in 15 minutes. If it runs out, use **Resend email**.

### What can go wrong

| What you see | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Wrong email or password — try again.** | One of the two is off. Check for a stray space at the end of your email. |
| **Too many attempts. Try again in 5 minutes.** | The system paused sign-in for a few minutes to keep the account safe. Wait it out. |
| **This setup link has expired. Ask your admin to resend the invitation.** | Your invite is too old. Ask the admin to send a new one. |
| **This link has already been used. Sign in or reset your password.** | You already set a password with that link. Just sign in normally. |

> ⚠️ Never share your password, and never sign in on someone else's behalf. Everything you do is recorded against your name — that record is what makes approvals mean something.

| **Task blocked** / **Still blocked: …** | Your task got marked blocked. If it stays blocked, the system checks every hour and nudges you again. |

**Where a nudge reaches you:**

- **In the app** — always. Every nudge is written to your bell, no exceptions.
- **By email** — for four types only: task due, task blocked, approval waiting and low stock. The subject line starts `[Konma]`.
- **On WhatsApp** — only if *all* of these are true: the admin has the WhatsApp switch on, you have a phone number saved, you've opted in, and it isn't quiet hours (9:00 pm to 7:00 am by default).

The system also holds a **cooldown** per nudge type so one stuck approval doesn't message you every hour. Approvals wait 24 hours between reminders, blocked tasks 12, low stock 4.

> ⚠️ WhatsApp nudges are **switched off across the whole villa** until the message templates are approved by Meta. Until then, the bell and email are how you'll hear about things.

> 💡 The **Operations** tab on the notifications page covers stock, orders, deliveries and admin notices. Shipment failures, morning briefs and daily-close reminders only show under **All** and **Unread** — so check **All** if you're hunting for one.

Old notifications are tidied away automatically after 30 days.

- **Board** shows lanes **To Do**, **Doing**, **Done** and **Blocked**. Drag a card between the first three.

Click any row or card to open the task.

## The task page

The heading is the task's title, with badges for its status, type and priority — and a green **Valid** badge once it's fully validated.

The page reads top to bottom:

- **Overview** — the description, the domain, the XP, the due date.
- **People** — who owns it.
- **Status** — this is where you move the work along.
- **Dependencies** — anything this task is waiting on.
- **Evidence** — the important one.

### Changing the status

In the **Status** section there's a dropdown with exactly three choices: **To Do**, **Doing**, **Done**. Pick one and it saves.

You can't set **Blocked** or **Cancelled** from that dropdown. If something is genuinely stuck, click **Report blocker** instead. A dialog opens: **Report a blocker** — *"What is blocking this task? Be specific so your admin can help resolve it."* Type at least a few words in **Blocker reason** (for example, *"Waiting for supplier confirmation on ingredient list"*) and click **Report blocker**. Only an admin can clear it again, with **Resolve blocker**.

> ⚠️ Moving a task to **Done** does *not* finish it. On its own, **Done** just means you've stopped working. The task isn't real progress until evidence is attached and approved.

### Adding evidence

Scroll to **Evidence**. If the task is yours (or you're an admin) you get a drop area reading **Drop files here, or click to browse** — *"Photos, documents, video up to 10 MB"* — and three buttons.

**1. Upload evidence** — a photo, a PDF, a Word document or a video. Best for anything visual: the restocked station, the plated dish, the signed delivery note.

**2. Add a link** — click it and a small form slides open under the buttons. Two boxes:
- the first one, showing `https://...`, is where the link goes;
- the second, **Add context for the reviewer**, is where you explain what the reviewer is looking at.
Click **Save link**. You'll see **Link evidence submitted.**

**3. Add a note** — one box: *"Describe what you did, including any relevant details..."* Click **Save note**. You'll see **Note submitted.**

> 💡 **Both of these are proper evidence.** A link and a note count exactly the same as a photo — the reviewer can approve any of the three. Use a note when there's genuinely nothing to photograph: a phone call made, a conversation had, a supplier chased.

Whatever you submit appears in the list below with a **Pending** badge until somebody reviews it.

### The Validation card

Above the upload area sits a small card. Before the task is finished it's headed **Validation required** with a ring reading `1/3` or `2/3` and three lines:

- **Status is Done**
- **At least one evidence approved**
- **All required approvals satisfied**

Each one ticks green as it's met. When all three are green the card flips to a **Valid** badge, the words **Task validated**, and a `3/3` ring. Confetti fires and a message tells you the XP you earned and the readiness points you added.

**That card is the whole system in miniature.** Three things, in that order, every time.

# ✅ Approvals — the two gates

This page only appears if your role is allowed to sign things off. The heading is **Approvals** with a count badge, and underneath: *"Everything waiting on your sign-off — tasks, recipes, decisions and evidence."*

Five tabs: **All**, **Tasks**, **Recipes**, **Decisions**, **Evidence**.

## The two things you are actually approving

A finished task normally needs **two different kinds of yes**, and both land in this same queue.

**Gate 1 — approving somebody's evidence.** Look at what they attached. Does the photo actually show the job done? Does the note actually explain it? You're answering one question: *is this real proof?* These rows sit under the **Evidence** tab.

**Gate 2 — the policy approval on the task itself.** Separately, the system checks a rule about *who must sign this kind of work off*. That rule is set by the task's domain. A food task, for example, needs both the Backend Lead and the Frontend Lead. An operations task needs the Founder/Admin. Those rows sit under the **Tasks** tab.

Both gates have to close before the task counts. That's deliberate: one person confirms the proof is genuine, and the right people confirm the work was the right thing to do.

Each row tells you what it needs. Look for the line that says **Single approver**, **Any 2 of the required roles** or **Every required role**.

## Deciding

Every row has the same two buttons, right there — no dialog to open.

- **Approve** — fires straight away. The row vanishes and you see **Approved.**
- **Reject** — opens a small panel *inside the row* asking **Why is this being sent back?** You must write **at least 10 characters**. The helper text says it plainly: *"They only see this note — make it enough to act on."* Then click **Send feedback**, or **Keep pending** to back out.

> 💡 A good rejection note is specific and actionable: *"The photo doesn't show the finished setup — retake it with the plated dish in frame."* A bad one is "no". The person has to redo the work from your note alone.

> ⚠️ You cannot approve your own work. If you try, the system stops you: **You cannot approve your own work**. That covers your own evidence *and* tasks you own. Somebody else always has to look. That's the whole point of the gate. (The Founder/Admin is the one exception, so the villa is never deadlocked.)

Nothing waiting? **No pending approvals** — *"You're all caught up."*

The Founder/Admin also has an **Override** button on each row, for emergencies. It requires a written reason and is permanently recorded.

# 📈 Readiness

The sidebar's **Readiness** link opens a page called **Readiness Intelligence**: *"Validated tasks and live operations data, blended into one number per meter."*

A **meter** is one honest score, 0 to 100, for one part of the villa. There are twelve:

**Villa Readiness** · **Backend Readiness** · **Frontend Readiness** · **Procurement Readiness** · **Standardization Readiness** · **Sales Readiness** · **Quality Readiness** · **BI Readiness** · **Tech Readiness** · **Talent Readiness** · **Art Experience Readiness** · **Lifestyle Experience Readiness**

They're grouped into two sections, and the page explains itself:

- **Derived from operations** — *"Computed from live ops data — catalog, procurement, sales and quality — and recomputed nightly. Hybrid meters blend that with validated tasks 50/50."*
- **Task-driven** — *"Moves only when a task is validated with approved evidence."*

Each meter is a ring with a percentage, its name, and a badge telling you how it moves:

| Badge | What moves it |
|---|---|
| **Task-driven** | Only a validated task with approved evidence. |
| **Derived** | The villa's live operations data, recalculated every night. |
| **Hybrid** | Half and half — the two are averaged 50/50. |

## How a meter actually moves

Click any ring and a panel opens below it.

For a **task-driven** meter, the panel lists **Contributing tasks** — the exact tasks that pushed the number up, who owns them and what each was worth. That list *is* the score. Nothing else touches it. When a task validates, its points get added and the meter recomputes on the spot. If that task is ever un-validated — say the evidence gets rejected on review — the points come straight back off.

For a **derived** meter, the panel shows the formula, when it last ran, and **Recent signals** — the orders, deliveries and recipe changes that fed it. Nightly.

There's also a **History** chart (**30d** / **90d**) with one dot per day, so you can see whether you're climbing or sliding.

> 💡 This is the number that matters. Everything else in the app exists to make this number honest.

# 🔁 The heart of it, in one chain

Everything in this app is one loop, repeated. Learn this and the rest is detail.

1. **Work happens.** Somebody restocks a station, tests a recipe, calls a vendor.
2. **A task records it.** The owner opens the task and moves the status to **Done**.
3. **Evidence gets attached.** A photo, a file, a link, or a note. Proof that it happened.
4. **Gate 1: someone approves the evidence.** A second pair of eyes says *yes, this is real proof.*
5. **Gate 2: the named roles approve the task.** The people the policy requires say *yes, this was the right work.*
6. **The task turns Valid.** The Validation card hits `3/3`. XP lands on the owner.
7. **The readiness meter rises.** If the task feeds a meter, its points are applied and the meter recomputes immediately.
8. **The villa's progress is real.** The number on **Readiness** is no longer an opinion. It's the sum of work that actually got done and actually got checked.

> ⚠️ Break the chain anywhere and it stops. A task marked **Done** with no evidence is worth nothing. Evidence with nobody to approve it is worth nothing. That's not bureaucracy — it's the only reason the readiness number can be trusted.

And it runs backwards too. Reject a piece of evidence on a task that had already validated, and the task loses **Valid**, the XP comes off, and the meter drops back down. Nothing is permanent until it's earned.

| **Bridge** | The part of the system that captures evidence automatically from orders, deliveries and prep batches, so you don't have to. |

# 🆘 If something looks wrong

- **A screen you expect is missing from the sidebar.** Your role probably doesn't have it. Ask the admin — they can grant it on the **Modules** screen in seconds.
- **You can see a page but a button is greyed out or absent.** Seeing a screen and being allowed to act on it are two different permissions. Ask the admin.
- **A page says it can't load.** Almost every screen has a **Retry** button. Try that first, then refresh.
- **Your XP hasn't gone up after finishing something.** Check the task's Validation card. You're probably waiting on an approval — chase the reviewer.
- **You're not getting WhatsApp nudges.** Ask the admin to check that your phone number is saved and the WhatsApp switch is on for you. In-app notifications always work regardless.

> 🏡 That's everything you need to start. Open **https://www.konma.store/team**, sign in, and look at **My Tasks**. The rest you'll learn by doing.
